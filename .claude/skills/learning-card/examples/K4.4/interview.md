## 1. The reader's question

**Asked:** Who asked you this recently, and how did they put it?

**Answer:** "The number looks wrong. Now what?", and more often not asked at all: the figure gets fixed in Excel. The quote in the card is the second, as a colleague says it.

**In the card:** Why now, the quote and the situation.

## 2. What went wrong

**Asked:** What went wrong, concretely? One metric from our dashboards, or one sentence a colleague said.

**Answer:** Two names that look alike get read as the same thing, the figures don't add up, and "the data is wrong" goes round in Slack or gets fixed quietly in Excel. The twist: half the time the data wasn't wrong; the reading was.

**In the card:** Core idea, "At Immoscoop"; the last sentence of Why now; the wrong answers of the first question.

## 3. The aha

**Asked:** What is the one thing that, once someone sees it, makes the rest obvious?

**Answer:** It starts with one question, "do I understand it?", and climbs a ladder: read the definition, ask the Owner, then raise a Signal.

**In the card:** Core idea, the rule.

## 4. The everyday anchor

**Asked:** Which everyday thing behaves exactly like this?

**Answer:** A smoke alarm, Claude's suggestion, which the author took and sharpened: whoever smells smoke must report it. Tested: it carries the check first (burnt toast) and the duty to say something (the next person smells it too).

**In the card:** Core idea, "Like a smoke alarm".

## 5. Never again, ask first

**Asked:** What may a colleague never do again after this card, and what do they ask first from now on?

**Answer:** Never fix it silently, never in a private message. Ask first: do I understand it? There is no such thing as a bad question: if it is unclear to you, it is unclear to your colleague.

**In the card:** You can now; the wrong answers of the first two questions; the third question.

## The ladder

1. **Story:** a comic of three to five panels: the smell, the toast, the call.
2. **Structure:** three steps: understand, ask, signal.
3. **Data Hub:** the Message on a Resource: Question, Remark, Signal, never private.

The card has no See it: the third rung is a screen of Data Hub, not a chart, and the Chart Server draws only charts. Apply leads to the Messages of a Resource instead. The first two rungs are pictures (Figma work); the card has no place for them yet.
