# Three example cards from the test rounds

The three cards of the interview with Max (2026-11-09), written in the shape Data Hub reads. Use them to calibrate tone and length, not as a template to fill in. Quotes and figures are illustrative: in a real card they come from questions 1 and 2, and the fact check confirms them. They are topics of the plan, not written cards: they are not in `packages/learning/cards/`, and the Learning Centre does not show them.

| Folder | Topic in the plan | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `2.1.3/` | Leading versus lagging indicators | A full card with See it as `calendarRatio`, in English and Dutch; the side entrance of question 1 |
| `1.2.7/` | State or movement | A card for Learning Level 1 next to a written card of Level 2 (2.3.6), See it as `calendarAverage` of a state; question 2 that gave theory first |
| `K4.4/` | Reporting an error or a doubt | A process card without See it: rung 3 is a screen, so `Apply:` leads there; an anchor Claude suggested and the author sharpened |

Each folder has `en.md` and `interview.md`; `2.1.3/` also `nl.md`. The tests of `packages/learning` read them with the parser of Data Hub, so that they keep the shape of a card when the format changes.
