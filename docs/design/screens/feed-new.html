Delivery

How often

When a run fails, nothing new is delivered: Applications keep the last good delivery, with its date, until the next run succeeds.

Live is refused for this query. One question reads up to 640 MB, above the 100 MB a live question may read, and it would run at every question of an Application. Its search keys do not narrow the partitions of Sales prices: a question for one street reads all 24 months.

Prepared, the same query runs once a day and every question reads the copy. Or make the query read less, such as fewer months, and come back.

Step 5 of 5

Who must say yes

Read from its Resources: every Owner Unit of a Resource this query reads. Nobody is asked now. Once an Application asks to read this Feed, each gets a Request in Messages with what leaves, and the Feed delivers nothing to it until all of them said yes.
