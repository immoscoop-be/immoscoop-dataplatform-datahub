1.2.2Unique counts don't add upI understand it
Depth

The report says 61,000 visitors last month, but its four weeks add up to 82,000. Which one is wrong?

A colleague from sales, on Monday

You will hear this question again, from sales, marketing or management, every time a month is put next to its weeks.

In this card: why the weeks never add up to the month.

A unique count counts everyone once per period, so the periods of a unique count never add up.

Like a guest list

Someone who comes on Friday and on Saturday is on both evenings' lists, but is one guest of the weekend.

For example

4,000 people visit on Monday and 3,000 on Tuesday. Together that is at least 4,000 and at most 7,000 people: whoever came on both days counts once.

Look at

the dashed part of the top bar: what the seven days count more than the week.

You see

3,700 visits by people who came on more than one day. Adding the days up counts them again, so the week is lower.

Predict first, then see why

In January 9,000 people sent a credit lead, in February 7,000. How many people sent one in the two months together?

Whoever sent a lead in both months counts once in the two together. So the total lies between the busier month and the sum of both.

Predict first, then see why

Which of these may you add up over the days of a month?

Page views are not unique: every view counts, so the days add up to the month. Unique visitors and unique listings published need a count over the whole month.

The footer of the monthly figure says "counted over the month". A dashboard that added the days up would count everyone who came back again.

Predict first, then see why

Each quarter about 300 agencies received a listing lead. Over the year the count says 420 agencies, not 1,200. What happened?

Both figures are right. A quarter counts an agency once per quarter, the year once in the year, so the year is lower than the sum.

Predict first, then see why

Which label tells you a figure is a unique count?

"Distinct" and "unique" mean each one counts once. Sessions and page views count every visit and every view.

You can now

recognise a unique count by its label, and explain why its days, weeks or months never add up.
