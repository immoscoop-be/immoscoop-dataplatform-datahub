1.2.2Unique counts don't add upMark as understood
What you will be able to do

Recognise a unique count by its label, and explain why a total over several periods is never the sum of those periods.

A unique count counts every person, or every listing, once. Someone who visits on three days of a week is one unique visitor in that week, but counts three times when you add up the days.

Where it goes wrong

  • Adding up the days to get the week or the month.
  • A dashboard that adds the days up when you switch to months.
  • Comparing a month with the sum of its weeks.

Signals to look for

  • "Unique" or "distinct" in the name or the label.
  • A total lower than the sum of its parts.
  • A figure that changes when you change the period.

One week of visitors: the seven days add up to 9,800, yet 6,100 people came.

Why unique visitors don't add up3:40 · captions

In practice Unique visitors per month, on the listing dashboard, is counted over the whole month. It is never the sum of the days.

Three right answers and you're through
Question 1

On Monday 4,000 people visited, on Tuesday 3,000. How many unique visitors were there on the two days together?

Someone who came on both days counts once in the two days together, so the total lies between the busiest day and the sum of the days.

Question 2

Which of these may you add up over the days of a month?

Page views are not unique: every view counts, so the days add up to the month. Unique visitors and unique listings published need a count over the whole month.

Question 3

The unique visitors of four weeks add up to 20,000. The unique visitors of the month are 14,000. What happened?

Both figures are right. The weeks count a returning visitor once per week, the month once in the month, so the month is lower.

Question 4

A dashboard shows unique visitors per day, and you switch it to months. What do you check?

Dashboard tools often add the days up when you switch from day to month. For a unique count, that counts everyone who came back again.

Question 5

Which label tells you a figure is a unique count?

"Distinct" and "unique" mean each one counts once. Sessions and page views count every visit and every view.
