Row count

What it does

It counts the rows, and sets them against the grain: how many weeks, how many lead sources, and which week–source pairs have no row.

Why it matters

A number of rows says little on its own. Against its grain it shows what is missing: a lead source without a row in some weeks counts as zero in every total, whether it had no leads or its load failed.
