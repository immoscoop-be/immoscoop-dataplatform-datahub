Distribution of numbers

What it does

It shows how the values of a number are spread: how many rows have small values and how many large, where the middle is, and how long the tail runs.

Why it matters

An average alone hides the shape. When a few rows bring many, the mean lies well above what most rows bring, and a report on the average tells a story that fits almost no row.
