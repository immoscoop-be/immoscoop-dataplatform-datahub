EDA-Probes drawn on a table

Business knows a table: rows, columns, a header. Every EDA-Probe is drawn on that same table, with fine lines and the kind of each field in its header, so that the drawing shows what the probe looks at and what a finding looks like. The drawing teaches the idea; the chart beside it shows the numbers of this Resource.
