No key of an Application is revoked.

An Application gets its key once one of its Feeds has the yes of every Owner under it; the key is shown once, on its page. Late and Held are no errors: the last good delivery is served, with its date in the answer. Errors are cut off by an Owner, not allowed (a revoked key, a token of another user pool, a Feed not consented), and Data Hub unavailable.
