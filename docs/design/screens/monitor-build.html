How to break it down

A breakdown is one query on Credit leads: per bucket of its time column, cut by up to three dimensions. Every metric and rule in it holds for each value, also a value that comes later: a new partner is covered without changing anything.

One bucket is
  • created_at is the partition column of Credit leads: a run reads only the days that are new or still settling, about 2 MB. The first run reads the past 12 weeks once, 38 MB, for the history the rules compare with.
  • updated_at is not the partition column of Credit leads: BigQuery cannot leave out the days before, so every run reads the whole table, 240 MB, above the free range. On created_at, the partition column, a run reads about 2 MB.

At 1.5 it would have fired 5 times for KBC in 12 weeks, 3 of them on a quiet weekend day, and 38 times across all 16 partners: likely more noise than you want. At 2 it would have fired 3 times for KBC in 12 weeks: on Saturday 26 September, and on 1 and 2 November, when the feed of KBC broke. Across all 16 partners, 21 times. At 2.5 it would have fired 3 times for KBC in 12 weeks, and 9 times across all 16 partners. At 3 it would have fired twice for KBC, on 1 and 2 November only, and 4 times across all 16 partners: the same as the rule that drops sharply.

What is a standard deviation? How far a day usually strays from the 28-day average of KBC. The band is that distance, twice, on both sides: a day outside it is unusual for KBC, judged against KBC itself, not against the large partners. Because Sundays are low and Mondays high, the band is wide; a rule on the same weekday a week back would be narrower. Reading your own figures

When this breakdown judges

By default after each of its buckets: a day here. Choose the moment that suits you; it need not follow the runs of dbt. Another breakdown of this monitor has its own schedule.

How often
On
  • Credit leads is stable at D-1: at 08:00 on Tuesday, this breakdown judges Monday. You do not set this; it follows its Stability.
  • If Credit leads was not refreshed by then, it judges the data of the run before, and says so on every finding, rather than see a drop that is not there.
  • One run reads about 2 MB, the days that are new or still settling, within the free range of 100 MB: about 50 MB a month on weekdays. The first run reads the past 12 weeks once, 38 MB.
