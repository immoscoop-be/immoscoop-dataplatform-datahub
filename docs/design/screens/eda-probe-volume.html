Volume over time, with empty periods

What it does

It counts what comes in per week, from the first week to this one, and finds the weeks without any row.

Why it matters

A week without rows is almost always a break: tracking that stopped, a load that failed. It looks like a quiet week, and every trend and comparison over it is off.

Parameters

Set on
week_startThe date its role names: its period.
Per
From its period, a week. Blocks of whole weeks from a Monday, so each holds the same weekdays; no month, as its days and weekends differ. The result shows every unit; this one is drawn.
Counts
May be empty
Optional, for a count per day, such as weekends. None for a week.
