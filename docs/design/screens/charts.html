Moving window average

movingWindowAverage

How does this evolve, without the daily noise?

For questions such as
  • How have credit leads evolved over the last four weeks?
  • Is the number of new listings per day going up or down?
Not for
  • Comparing with last year, or the period before: Comparison with the previous period
  • One value as it is now: KPI tile
What it needs

The blocks computed in BigQuery, at least 28 of them; semgraph draws, it never computes over rows.

Where you meet it

In Claude, which asks semgraph for it by name; later on a Lens or a Report. Anyone may draw it at POST /render with "semgraph": "movingWindowAverage".
