What it does

It finds the first and the last week_start, how many weeks lie between, and sets the last against today: is the table up to date?

Why it matters

A table that stopped three weeks ago looks the same as one loaded this morning. Every report on it shows a quiet last month, and nobody sees that the load stopped.
