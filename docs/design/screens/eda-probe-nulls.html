Nulls per Key Field

What it does

It counts, per field, the rows where nothing is filled in, and what share of all rows that is.

Why it matters

An empty value drops out of every count by that field and every average over it, without anyone seeing it. In a field its model type needs, such as the week of a weekly table, a row without it cannot be placed at all.
