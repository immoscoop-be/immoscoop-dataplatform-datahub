From exposure to lens

How data comes into Data Hub and becomes a lens for the whole company, in seven steps. Each step says who does it and opens the screens it happens on, seen as that role.

Data team Owner Colleagues
  1. The data team makes it available
  2. 1

    The data team exposes a table in dbt

    Data team

    An exposure with the tag datahub in the dbt project. Merging it is the registration: every table it depends on arrives in Data Hub as a resource, still without a collection.

    exposures:
  - name: datahub_credit_leads
    type: application
    owner: { name: Data Team }
    tags: ['datahub']
    depends_on:
      - ref('weekly_lead_volume')
      - ref('lead_sources')
    A resource without a collection
  3. 2

    The data team adds it to a collection

    Data team

    From the page of a collection, a data admin picks the resources without a collection. From then on they belong to the owner unit of that collection, and only it and the data team see them.

    The page of a collection Adding resources
  4. The owner takes it over
  5. 3

    The owner takes control

    Owner

    The page of the collection tells the owner what waits for them: resources not published yet, a level that dropped, open signals.

    Collections The page of a collection, as owner
  6. 4

    The owner checks the data and describes it

    Owner

    With the preview, the owner looks at the first rows. On the page of the resource, they describe it and its fields: what it holds, how it behaves, its gaps and its key fields.

    Preview The page of a resource Describing it
  7. 5

    The owner publishes the resource

    Owner

    Publishing is the first confirmation: the resource rises to level 3, Confirmed, and everyone who sees the collection finds it.

    Publication
  8. The company works with it
  9. 6

    The owner and colleagues query it

    OwnerColleagues

    "Query" on a resource starts a new query with its name filled in. First the lenses that already answer the question; otherwise their own SQL, with other resources they may see.

    "Query" on a resource A new query The query
  10. 7

    A query becomes a lens

    Owner

    When a query answers a question that comes back, "Make it a lens" turns it into a lens. Its size is checked: within range it is published at once, a large one gets a warning, a very large one waits for the data team. Then everyone who may use its resources finds it.

    "Make it a lens" Within range Large Very large
