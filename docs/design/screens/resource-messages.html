Description

Business descriptionFrom the owner

The number of credit leads we send to KBC each week, per lead source. Use it to follow the trend and to compare sources; it counts leads, not people.

DescriptionFrom dbt

One row per week and lead source with the number of credit leads sent to KBC. A lead counts in the week it was first submitted, not when it was followed up. Test and duplicate requests are excluded.

model type: martgrain: week × lead sourceFrom the data team
