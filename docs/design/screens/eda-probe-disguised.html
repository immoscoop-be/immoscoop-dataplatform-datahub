Disguised nulls

What it does

It looks, per field, for values that fill a cell but stand for nothing, such as "n/a", "unknown" or 1900-01-01, and counts the rows that hold them.

Why it matters

A null is seen as empty; "unknown" is counted as a value. It becomes a lead source of its own in every report, and a date of 1900 pulls every average of an age or a duration.
