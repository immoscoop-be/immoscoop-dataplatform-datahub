1.2.2Unique counts don't add up

Messages2 Share in a message Mark as understood UnderstoodUndo
1 · User Required Apply 6 min read Video 3:40 3 questions
What you will be able to do

Recognise a unique count by its label, and explain why a total over several periods is never the sum of those periods.

A unique count counts every person, or every listing, once. Someone who visits on three days of a week is one unique visitor in that week, but counts three times when you add up the three days.

Counts that are not unique, such as page views or leads, you may add up: every view and every lead counts. "Average number of unique visitors per day" is correct too, but it is a different figure than the unique visitors of the month.

The common mistake

Reading or building a monthly total as the sum of the days. Dashboard tools often do this for you when you switch from days to months.

At Immoscoop

  • Unique visitors per month, counted over the whole month.
  • Unique listings published per month.
  • Unique agencies that received a listing lead in a quarter.
  • Unique people who sent a credit lead, per month and per year.

Right. Someone who came on both days counts once in the two days together, so the total lies between the busiest day and the sum of the days.

Not quite. Page views are not unique: every view counts, so the days add up to the month. Unique visitors and unique listings published need a count over the whole month.
