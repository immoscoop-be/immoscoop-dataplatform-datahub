Long names

Our names often say what matters last: current (flat), monthly, the date of a query, the table after its dataset. A browser cuts a name at its end, which cuts exactly that. Four ways to fit a long name, in CSS alone, on names from uat. Drag the lower right corner of a column to make it narrower or wider; every name shows in full on hover.
