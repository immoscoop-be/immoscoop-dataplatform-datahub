## To be worked out

Three themes still need their own elaboration. They are connected: the Catalogue is where permissions are tied to and where the Promotion Loop ends up, so it comes first.

### 1. Catalogue management

The Catalogue is layered. Whoever asks a question expects a chart or an action; whoever digs deeper sees that the answer is built from Resources that are combined, aggregated and enriched. What the Catalogue shows therefore goes from Resources, through Lenses, to Reports.

- <a id="dec-dh-60"></a>**DEC-DH-60** · Business does not need to know the concept "intermediate". It works with Resources, makes Lenses and new concepts from them itself, and brings those together in Reports.
- <a id="dec-dh-61"></a>**DEC-DH-61** · A new concept that business defines itself, such as buy session, is Beta: it lives only in its own context in Data Hub and is at Level 0 of the ladder, regardless of the Level of the Resources beneath it. What is built on it is Beta too. *Note of 2026-10-28: a Beta Concept is a Lens or a query, and is shared as one ([DEC-DH-70](#dec-dh-70)).* *Note of 2026-10-28: a Lens or query that defines one shows the lowest Level of its Resources; Level 0 is abstract ([DEC-DH-72](#dec-dh-72)).*
- <a id="dec-dh-62"></a>**DEC-DH-62** · There is no intermediate "alpha" step. A concept that is hardened comes back as a Resource and is then on the normal ladder, from Described. *Note of 2026-10-28: from 1 · Available, as every Resource ([DEC-DH-72](#dec-dh-72)).*
- <a id="dec-dh-70"></a>**DEC-DH-70** · A Beta Concept is a Lens or a query: business defines it in the SQL of either. It is shared as a Lens or a query is, with no feature of its own: a Lens with everyone who sees its Collection ([DEC-CO-20](../requirements/functional/03-collections.md#dec-co-20)), a query as [DEC-SR-31](../requirements/functional/04-stacks-and-resources.md#dec-sr-31) says. Hardening it is the Data Team taking that SQL and building a dbt model from it; with an exposure it becomes a Resource on the normal ladder ([DEC-DH-62](#dec-dh-62), [FR-SR-32](../requirements/functional/04-stacks-and-resources.md#fr-sr-32)). This is already in the current scope, implicitly. *Decided on 2026-10-28, by Max ([OP-DH-07](#op-dh-07)).*
- <a id="dec-dh-72"></a>**DEC-DH-72** · A Lens, a Report and a query take the lowest Level of the Resources they are built on; that is their Level, also when they define a Beta Concept. Level 0, Beta, is abstract: it says only that something is used within Data Hub and does not formally exist. Once someone asks to make it formal, the Data Team hardens it ([DEC-DH-70](#dec-dh-70)), and its Resource climbs the ladder from 1 · Available, then 2 · Described, and so on. *Decided on 2026-10-28, by Max ([OP-DH-08](#op-dh-08)).*

### 3. The Promotion Loop

Conceptually described in [Domain tooling](https://immoscoop.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/DATA/pages/1351975041) (Confluence, Dutch); practically still open. In ladder terms: a Beta Concept is hardened into a Resource, with the beta query as its specification. The librarian puts it on the shelves; it starts at Described and climbs again, with a new EDA and monitoring.

- [x] <a id="op-dh-26"></a>**OP-DH-26** · Work out where and how promotion happens technically, from a concept in Data Hub to a Resource in the Stacks. *Decided on 2026-10-28, by Max: the Data Team takes the SQL of the Lens or query and builds a dbt model from it, which an exposure makes a Resource ([DEC-DH-70](#dec-dh-70)).*
